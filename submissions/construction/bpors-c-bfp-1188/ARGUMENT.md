# BPORS-C+BFP at 1,188 bytes

Each of four coordinates selects one of 1,024 buckets and opens four of its 24 leaf chains. The four residual depths sum to 32 per coordinate. Each bucket path has ten siblings, and the four child proofs together may have at most 18 siblings. The proposed maximum is 4 counter bytes plus 16 × (4 × (4 + 10) + 18) = 1,188 bytes. The row uses 145 raw bits and a 29-bit counter. Its counting argument gives at most 9,816,603 expected signing hashes and 219 hashes per successful verification. Lean verifies the row arithmetic, the pruned support count and the finite Poisson sum including a bound on the tail. It also checks the four coefficients used to count suppliers for the target depths. For every history length, coverage by the pooled disclosures must satisfy the counting bound. The submitted Lean theorem derives the full challenge goal assuming those properties.
